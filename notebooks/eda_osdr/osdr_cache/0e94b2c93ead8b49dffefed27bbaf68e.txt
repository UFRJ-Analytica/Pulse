One piece was placed in a cuvette and immersed in Phosphate buffered saline. This tissue, which served as the Earth Control tissue sample, was kept in an incubator at 30C for the whole mission duration. This temperature was equivalent to that found on the ISS. The other piece of tissue was placed in another cuvette and immersed in PBS. The six cuvettes containing the tissue samples were mounted on the IceCube and sent to Cape Canaveral for a SpaceX CRS23 re-supply mission. These will be referred to as the ISS tissue samples. After returning back to Earth, both the samples that were sent to ISS and the control samples were kept at 15-18C in Phosphate buffered saline (the solution that they were in during the experiment). Both the ISS and Earth control tissues were kept at this temperature for a month until the ISS samples arrived in our labs in Malta from the USA.
